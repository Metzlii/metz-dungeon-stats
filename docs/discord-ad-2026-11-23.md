:bar_chart: Metz Dungeon Stats — https://greasyfork.org/en/scripts/603399

One permanent record of your dungeon runs, keys, chests, loot and XP, plus a live damage meter and party supply watch. Tracked from the game's own messages, nothing estimated.

:crossed_swords: Damage meter: live under your battle cards — damage, DPS, damage taken and healing for the whole party. Click a player for every ability, damage taken by monster, healing by source, and mana spent and gained.
:key: Keys in the battle window: your entry and chest keys with how long they last, and a warning naming the party member who runs out first.
:doughnut: Party consumables: everyone's food and drinks with counts and time left — hover your own slots, or open the Consumables tab for the whole party.
:stopwatch: Runs: every clear and fail with your average run time, including the runs made while you were offline.
:hammer_pick: Key planner: crafted, bought, used and held per key, cost per key, and a Buy button that opens the market with the amount filled in.
:package: Chests and loot: every opening with its contents and value, what to sell, and spent, sold and net for the whole record.
:chart_with_upwards_trend: XP: per skill, XP/hr, a table of days, and ability XP with the books and price to the next level.
:scroll: Lifetime: the game's Loot & XP Log, kept for good. Already on Toolasha? Its saved sessions and market history are pulled in.
:gear: Yours to arrange: drag tabs, columns and cards, click a header to sort, drag the line between players and monsters, Ctrl+click a row to chat.
:globe_with_meridians: In every game language, on dungeons and planets alike.

Getting Started
Install from Greasy Fork and reload the game. Open the Dungeon Stats tab beside Combat Zones, Find Party and My Party; the meter and keys appear on the Battle tab. On an old PC? One checkbox turns the meter off.

Read-only: it never sends anything to the game or anywhere else.

Something look off? Mail Metzli in-game (or here) with an export from the Data tab.
