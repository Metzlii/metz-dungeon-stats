:crossed_swords: Metz Dungeon Stats — Damage Meter — https://greasyfork.org/en/scripts/603399

A damage meter built from scratch on the game's own battle messages. Every wave is checked: each monster seen down to 0 HP and every point credited to a player, shown as a ✓ on the meter.

:bar_chart: Live in the Battle tab: damage, share, DPS, damage taken, healing done, healed and deaths for every player in your party.
:repeat: All runs by default — one running total for as long as the party stays in combat. One click switches to This run.
:mag: Click a player for the details: each ability's damage, DPS, uses, hits and misses.
:shield: Damage taken by monster and by the ability it used, with thorns, bleeds and HP costs each in their own row.
:herb: Healing by source: heal abilities, Life Drain, Bloom procs, regeneration ticks and your own food by name.
:stopwatch: Combat time runs from the first wave to the last hit, the time between waves included, so DPS reflects how your triggers actually play out.
:scroll: Damage tab: every full run you play is saved, plus totals per dungeon and tier. A reload mid-run is stitched back together.
:ringed_planet: Works on planets too.
:turtle: On an old PC? One checkbox turns the meter off completely.

Getting Started
Install from Greasy Fork and reload the game. The meter appears under your battle cards on the Battle tab; Dungeon Stats → Damage keeps every run.

Read-only: it never sends anything to the game or anywhere else.

Something look off? Mail Metzli in-game (or here) with a screenshot of the meter.
