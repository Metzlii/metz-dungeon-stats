:bar_chart: Metz Dungeon Stats — https://greasyfork.org/en/scripts/603399

One permanent record of your dungeon runs, keys, chests, loot and XP. Tracked from the game's own messages, nothing estimated.

:stopwatch: Runs: every clear and fail with your average run time, including the runs made while you were offline.
:key: Keys: crafted, bought, used and held per key type, cost per key, and a crafting planner with marketplace links.
:package: Chests: every opening with its contents and value, totals per chest type.
:moneybag: Loot: what to sell with a Sell button per item, and spent, sold and net for the whole record.
:chart_with_upwards_trend: XP: per skill since the record began, XP/hr, a table of days, and ability XP with the books and price to the next level.
:scroll: Lifetime: the game's own Loot & XP Log, kept for good — clears, chests, time and XP/hr per dungeon.
:speech_balloon: Ctrl+click any row or card to drop it into chat for your party.
:ringed_planet: Built for dungeon parties, works on planets too: XP, XP/hr, consumables used and the loot from every session are recorded in any combat zone.

Already running Toolasha? Your history comes with you.
:link: Loot & XP Log: every session Toolasha has saved is pulled in automatically, so Lifetime reaches back long before you installed — clears, chests, time, XP/hr and keys crafted.
:receipt: Market history: the keys and stock you already held are priced at what you actually paid, and the buys and sells Toolasha saw are added to the record. A history file from another PC merges in too.

Getting Started
Install from Greasy Fork and reload the game. Open the Dungeon Stats tab beside Combat Zones, Find Party and My Party. Playing on two PCs? Export on one, import on the other, and nothing counts twice.

Read-only: it never sends anything to the game or anywhere else.

Something look off? Mail Metzli in-game (or here) with an export from the Data tab.
